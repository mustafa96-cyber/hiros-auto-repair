# Hiro's Auto Repair, owner asset request and confirmation list

Prepared by Saedi Tech, September 13, 2026. Nothing below blocks the demo. All of it is needed before a real launch.

## Facts to confirm with the owner

* Suite number: Birdeye lists Ste D, the current official site omits it. Which is correct?
* Owner name and whether it may appear on the site.
* Years in business and founding year.
* Exact service list. The demo shows routine maintenance, brakes, tires, diagnostics, and major repairs based on public reputation signals. Confirm, add, or remove.
* Which Japanese makes are accepted, and whether Acura, Lexus, Infiniti, Subaru, and Mitsubishi should be listed.
* Whether Japanese language service is offered and may be stated.
* Whether the shop is family run and whether that may be stated.
* Any certifications, smog capability, warranties, towing, or payment methods.
* Permission to display the Yelp review count and Birdeye rating on the live site.
* Email destination for appointment form requests.
* Domain plan: keep hiros-auto.com and point it at the new site, or register a fresh domain.

## Assets requested from the owner

* Logo in vector or high resolution, or permission for Saedi Tech to design a mark.
* Storefront and signage photos.
* Service bay and workspace photos.
* A photo of the owner or team at work.
* Any proof documents for licenses or certifications to be named on the site.
* Links to any official social accounts.

## Phone camera shot list, about 30 minutes at the shop

1. Storefront straight on, morning light, roll up door open.
2. Signage close up.
3. One bay with a car on the lift, shot from the doorway.
4. Hands on a torque wrench or ratchet, close up.
5. The parts shelf or tool chest, tidy.
6. Owner standing at the bay entrance, arms relaxed.
7. A customer car front three quarter view, badge visible.
8. The office counter where customers check in.

Shoot horizontal, wipe the lens first, avoid harsh midday shadows.
